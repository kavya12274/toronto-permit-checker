# Demo video script: Permit Pre-Check (about 55 to 60 seconds)

Read each numbered line, then **pause about 1 second** before the next one. The pauses tell me where each line starts, so the screen recording can line up with your voice.

| # | You say | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Want to build an addition or a garden suite in Toronto? Finding out if it's allowed means reading hundreds of pages of rules." | Home page: "Can you build it?" |
| 2 | "Permit Pre-Check does it for you. It checks your project against Toronto's zoning, Building Code basics and other approvals." | The "What it checks" box: 174 rules |
| 3 | "Pick your project and type your address. It looks up the City's zoning map for you." | Step 1: a project card is tapped. Step 2: address typed, Look up fills in the values |
| 4 | "Add your measurements. Skip one, and it says 'need more info', never a pass." | Step 4: numbers typed, "Don't know" tapped |
| 5 | "Every result shows pass or fail, the by-law section, and the smallest fix. Then print a report for your designer." | Results: summary tiles, a FAIL card with its fix, then the printable report |
| 6 | "Next, I'm adding AI that reads your drawings to fill in the form, and checks if your design fits the neighbourhood. The rules still decide pass or fail, not the AI." | "Coming soon" cards: AI plan reader, then Vibes check |
| 7 | "Then more zones, other cities, and past variance decisions nearby." | A "What's next" card listing those three |
| 8 | "It's a pre-check, not an approval, but it turns hours of reading into minutes." | Back to the home page |

About 145 words, which is about 55 to 60 seconds at a normal speaking pace.

## Recording tips
- Use Voice Memos on your phone or Mac, in a quiet room, about a hand's width from the microphone.
- Read the lines as written, so the screen actions match what you're saying. Small slips are fine.
- Pause about 1 second between lines.
- Send me the file (an .m4a from Voice Memos is perfect).
